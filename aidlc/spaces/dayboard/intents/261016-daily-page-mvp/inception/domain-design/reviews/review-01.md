### Summary

The structure is sound and matches the confirmed answers. ADR-007 holds for FR3.11, FR4.9, NFR5 and NFR10 in a single backend process: the hash check and the DayChange record share one transaction, and LiveUpdates reads only through Storage. The weak points are in the multi-process case. The privacy guard has no stated reach over a second MCP process, and the note and learning state rules can be bypassed through a direct DayPages write. Both are worth settling before approving; the Minor items can wait for Contract Design or NFR Design.
