### Summary

The contracts match every confirmed answer (Q1 to Q7 and the summary). The privacy guard sits in front of HTTP, the stream and /mcp as the domain decisions require. The operation set covers the FR1 to FR4 rules, and the ownership rules are stated. Two gaps should be settled before approval: adding a block and its first item in one change has no way to name the new block, and the web-side slot and hook between U3, U4 and U5 does not carry the highlight, toast and calendar updates the components expect. The remaining findings are smaller contract details that Functional Design can absorb.
